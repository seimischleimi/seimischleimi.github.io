feedback rules

feedback should be given in a way that the other person can understand it.

adopting a meta-level perspective —> this means seeing things from an equal standpoint. I am no worse, and for sure, also no better, than others.

timing of feedback. You shouldn't give feedback a month later or after half a year. Instead, give it as soon as possible so that the other person hasn't forgotten what it's actually about.

it also doesn't hurt to check with the other person to see if they're even open to feedback. “I noticed that... ... is it okay if I say something about that?”

These are ideals. That means it would be good if things were that way. Unfortunately, I catch myself making mistakes in this area, too. Still, it would be better to keep these in mind, and it doesn't hurt to remind myself of them every now and then. And if you're not sure you can pull it off, you might as well just shut up.
